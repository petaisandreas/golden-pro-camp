How do I become part of Golden?

Golden is built intentionally. We want every edition to bring together women who will both benefit from the environment and contribute to it.

For that reason, participation is through an application and selection process.

  1. Apply

    Tell us about yourself, your competitive journey, your goals and what you’re working towards.

  2. Be reviewed

    Applications are reviewed individually. Selection is based on competitive level, achievements and what we believe will add value to the group.

  3. Get your invitation

    Selected athletes will receive an invitation by email to accept their spot in the next Golden.
