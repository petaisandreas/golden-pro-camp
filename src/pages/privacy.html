Privacy Policy

Who we are

Golden Pro is a female training camp run by Eleftheria Christodoulou in Larnaca, Cyprus. You can reach us by email at {{ contact.email }}, on WhatsApp at {{ contact.whatsapp }}, or on Instagram at {{ contact.instagramHandle }}.

What we collect

Only what you type into the application form: your name, age, belt, email, Instagram, country, WhatsApp number, achievements and the text about yourself. If you are under 18, we also collect your parent’s or guardian’s name and contact details.

We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking. The fonts on this site are hosted on our own site.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

To review your application and contact you about the camp. Our legal basis is your consent and steps taken at your request before an agreement (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and (b)).

Where it is stored

The form is sent to Google Forms and stored in a Google Sheet that only the Golden organisers can access. When you send it, Google also receives technical data such as your IP address and browser type. Google acts as our processor and may store data outside the EU under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We delete applications no later than 12 months after the camp you applied for, unless you join a later camp and ask us to keep it.

Under 18

We accept applications from under-18s only with a parent’s or guardian’s agreement. We contact the parent or guardian and ask for a signed consent form before a minor takes part.

Photos

Camp photos on this site are published with the athletes’ permission. If you appear in a photo and want it removed, contact us.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw your consent at any time, object to how we use it, or receive it in a portable format. Contact us and we will reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection, Cyprus (www.dataprotection.gov.cy).

Changes

We update this page if anything changes.
