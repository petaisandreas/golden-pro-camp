Founder of Golden

Eleftheria
Christodoulou

  • IBJJF European double champion
  • IBJJF Pan champion
  • ADCC Trials winner & ADCC veteran

I grew up training different sports, from karate and volleyball to Olympic weightlifting. Movement, training and competition were always part of my life, but it wasn’t until I discovered Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in 2016 that I found something I was willing to completely change my life for.

At the time, I was studying Architecture in Cyprus. A few years later after graduation, I left my career, my home and everything comfortable to move to a small city in Ukraine, because I wanted to understand what could happen if I gave myself the environment, training partners and support to truly pursue my potential as an athlete.

That decision eventually took me from a small island with a very small grappling scene to winning the ADCC European Trials and competing at the ADCC World Championships.

But somewhere along that journey, my ambition became bigger than my own competitive career.

I realised how much the right environment can change an athlete — and how difficult it can be for women to find that environment consistently.

Golden grew from the desire to create it.

A place where ambitious women can find the training partners, opportunities, support and community that help them believe bigger, train harder and see what they are truly capable of.
