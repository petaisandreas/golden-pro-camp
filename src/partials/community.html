What is Golden?

Golden is an environment built and focused around the development of female competitors.

We bring ambitious competitors together and give them something that can be difficult to find in their everyday training: each other.

Different bodies. Different games. Different styles. Women who expose problems in your Jiu-Jitsu, force you to adapt and give you rounds you may not have access to at home.

The training is designed around learning through problems, exploration and live resistance. We use an ecological approach to create situations where athletes have to read, adapt, make decisions and discover solutions against opponents who are trying to do the same.

We want to create the conditions around the athlete too — recovery, accommodation, good food, rest, connection, opportunities to compete, access to people within the sport, and time away from the mats to actually enjoy being together.

Because becoming a better athlete is not only about doing more rounds.

It is also about who you meet, what you are exposed to, and what you start believing is possible for yourself.

That is why Golden brings women from different academies and countries together without asking them to stop being competitors.

  • We face each other at major tournaments.
  • We still train together.

Share knowledge. Challenge each other. Build friendships. Create opportunities for each other. And then go back to our own teams stronger than when we arrived.

The camp lasts a few days.
The network shouldn’t.

Golden is about building an international community of female competitors who continue to train, compete, connect and push each other forward long after they leave Cyprus.

02

Who is Golden for?

Golden is for women who are competitors and also serious about their development.

You don’t need to be a world champion. You do necessarily need to arrive willing to train, learn, contribute, challenge yourself and add something positive to the room.

Some women will arrive with years of international experience. Others will still be working towards their first major breakthrough.

Ambition is what connects them.

Train together. Grow together. Raise the level together.
